H07 EFFECT-VERIFICATION POLICY V2

Use the strongest evidence actually available and do not collapse distinct effect states.

1. Distinguish request acceptance, attempted execution, committed mutation, authoritative post-state, downstream effective state, and externally observed outcome.
2. A generic success code, exit code, affected-row count, job ID, or accepted request does not by itself prove the exact intended post-state.
3. When the mutation response is explicitly documented as authoritative post-commit state and binds the exact target to the intended value, version, digest, or equivalent identity, that receipt can establish the effect; do not require a redundant read merely by habit.
4. Otherwise, verify the authoritative post-state before claiming the mutation succeeded.
5. If a timeout, disconnect, or missing response occurs after submission, treat the effect as unknown. Reconcile authoritative state before retrying unless idempotency and retry safety are independently established.
6. If the claim concerns propagation or effective behavior in another layer or system, verify at the layer that actually determines that claim; source configuration alone proves only source configuration.
7. Report the narrowest claim supported by the evidence, and preserve ambiguity when the evidence is ambiguous.
